_Generated from [provinces-sub-saharan-africa.csv](provinces-sub-saharan-africa.csv) (batch 127). Same columns and rules as the North America, Europe, Latin America, East Asia and Oceania and South Asia slices, including `contested` and `linked_cities` (batch 122). Population follows the spec formula from rough 2019 population estimates (A); Commerce and Industry are hand-set (T). Names are ASCII (A). Resource tags include Bauxite, Silver, Zinc and Tin (batch 124). Every value is in USD; currencies are not modelled (batch 125). Alternate history (19-history, 7-starting-world): no COVID and no 2008 crash; Africa starts weak and coup-prone with Lagos as the one hub that can rise; Mali has been a US debt vassal since 2012 (a US base at Bamako, the debt office); there was no 2021 to 2023 coup wave, so Mali, Burkina Faso, Guinea, Niger, Chad and Gabon keep their January 2020 governments; Sudan is in a US-friendly transition after Bashir; Abiy rules Ethiopia; DR Congo is a major country. Insurgencies that began before 2020 (Boko Haram, the Sahel jihadists, al-Shabaab, Cabo Delgado, the anglophone Cameroon war, eastern Congo's militias) are in notes as in real life (A)._

## Things that work differently in Sub-Saharan Africa

- **No platinum or diamond tag.** Southern Africa's largest mining districts (Rustenburg, the Great Dyke, Jwaneng, Orapa, Catoca, Letseng) get no resource bonus. Recommendation: add Platinum (Mining) and Diamonds (Mining; Luxury) tags, which would also serve Russia (Norilsk, Yakutia) (A).
- **Mining-heavy provinces break the City band.** Kolwezi-Lualaba (52) and Lubumbashi-Likasi (51) sit above 45 because Industry 20 to 28 reflects about 70% of world cobalt. Recommendation: keep the values and let resource provinces of a key city exceed the band (as with the Permian Basin rule in 2.3.1) (T).
- **Huge single-state provinces.** Kano (15M), Kaduna, Katsina and Oyo hold whole Nigerian states; they reach Population 22 to 24 with low Commerce, as ruled for populous rural provinces (batch 127).
- **Chinese lending without vassalage.** countries.csv makes no African country a Chinese debt vassal, though Djibouti, Zambia, Congo-Brazzaville, Angola and Kenya carry the heaviest Belt and Road debt in real 2020; notes mention it only. Recommendation: Logan decides whether Djibouti or Zambia should start as a CHN subject (4.11.8), which would also give China a Bab-el-Mandeb foothold (A).
