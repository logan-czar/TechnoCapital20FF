### company-start.csv

651 rows, one per companies.csv row in the same order (Logan, batch 161; mvp-spec 7.1, G6), written by gen.py from companies.csv, industries.csv, country-economy.csv and financial-zones.csv. The starting balance sheet: asset book is 50% of gross business assets (1a); cash fills the gap up to book equity, or, when book equity is below the asset book, a drawn credit line covers the gap plus min_cash (5% of revenue). Opening prices vary by size (2b): `open_price = clamp(round(25 × (engine value ÷ $1B)^0.35 × jitter), 5, 200)`, jitter up to ±10% from a hash of the id (T), and `shares = engine value ÷ open_price`. validate.py checks ids, whole-dollar prices of 5 to 200, assets = liabilities + equity, book equity equal to companies.csv book value, asset book 50% of gross, no line drawn while holding more than min_cash, and shares × open_price = engine value.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| id, ticker, public | As in companies.csv |
| gross_assets_usd | Gross business assets of the 7.1 loadout (revenue × capital_per_dollar × 100 ÷ (100 + PC bonus); $100,000 for the barebones loadout) |
| asset_book_usd | 50% of gross (A) |
| cash_usd | book equity − asset book, or min_cash when the line is drawn |
| credit_drawn_usd | 0, or asset book − book equity + min_cash |
| book_equity_usd | cash + asset book − credit drawn; equals companies.csv book_value_usd_m |
| engine_value_usd | mvp-spec 5.13 fair value at load: flat synthetic history, Normal cycle, company confidence 1.0, X from the zone confidence_start (listed only), line interest skipped, floored at 0.6 × book and $100,000 (A) |
| open_price | Opening price per share in whole dollars, $5 to $200 (T) |
| shares | engine_value_usd ÷ open_price, rounded; listed and private alike |
