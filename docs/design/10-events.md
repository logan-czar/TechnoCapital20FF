# 10. Events and event chains

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Full rules: [detailed/13-events.md](detailed/13-events.md)._

- **Events** are data-driven cards with triggers, weights, cooldowns and 2 to 4 options.
- **Chains** drive founding paths, takeovers, scandals and quest lines ([detailed/8-quest-lines.md](detailed/8-quest-lines.md)).
- **The director** paces events and directs AI characters.
- Time auto-pauses on events and chosen alerts ([09-time-and-turns.md](09-time-and-turns.md)).
