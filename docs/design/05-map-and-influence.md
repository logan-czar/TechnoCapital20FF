# 5. Map and influence

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Full rules: [detailed/2-map-and-influence.md](detailed/2-map-and-influence.md)._

- **Map.** A 2D map of the real modern world in Godot, with world, country and hub zoom levels. Fictional companies, people and brands.
- **Places.** Countries, regions, economic hubs and districts. Finance and tech hubs give location perks.
- **Influence.** Not a skill: the amount a character has in an area, mainly from assets owned there, modified by birthplace and boosted by psyops or ads.
- **Strata.** Population is counted by strata, and votes are counted by strata.
- **Real estate** is pooled per district (commercial, residential, industrial, land).
- **Travel.** Location matters; private jets and travel stay.
