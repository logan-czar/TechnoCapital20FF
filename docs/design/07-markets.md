# 7. Markets

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74; Batch 125 (Logan): every value is in USD in the simulation; local currencies are flavor only; FX is post-POC; one world base rate from the Federal Monetary Board). Full rules: [detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md)._

- **Trading.** Stocks trade 24/7, closed only on 1 January. All quarterly earnings release at 00:00 on 2 Jan, 2 Apr, 2 Jul and 2 Oct, which makes those days a frenzy.
- **Prices.** Fair value blends book value with an industry earnings multiple, moved by news and investor confidence. Only named holders trade: funds and retail platforms.
- **Investing.** Stocks, bonds, real estate pools, private stakes and gambling positions; hedge, PE and VC funds; personal margin and shorts. Commodities come later. FX trading is post-POC (batch 125).
- **One unit of account (Logan, batch 125).** Every simulated value is in US dollars and every exchange lists in dollars; there are no exchange rates or FX trading in the POC (FX is post-POC). Countries keep their real currencies as flavor in news, history and UI text ("euro crisis", "yen weakens"). The Federal Monetary Board sets one world base rate from US data (3% at start **(T)**; batch 130); each country's government bonds price at that rate plus its sovereign spread, and companies borrow at it plus their home spread (half their financial zone's credit spread, half their home sovereign spread; batch 156) plus a rating spread. Other central banks are flavor names and political posts that set no rate **(A)**. In a banking crisis the Board can lend to a foreign country's banks only if that country is Friendly with the US **(T)** ([detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md) 3.8.1).
- **Information.** Accurate market data comes only from the Pdoomberg terminal ([11-ui.md](11-ui.md) 11.1.1).
