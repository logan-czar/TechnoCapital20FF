# 7. Markets

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74; Batch 124 card (Logan): whole world dollarized at start, no FX at start). Full rules: [detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md)._

- **Trading.** Stocks trade 24/7, closed only on 1 January. All quarterly earnings release at 00:00 on 2 Jan, 2 Apr, 2 Jul and 2 Oct, which makes those days a frenzy.
- **Prices.** Fair value blends book value with an industry earnings multiple, moved by news and investor confidence. Only named holders trade: funds and retail platforms.
- **Investing.** Stocks, bonds, real estate pools, private stakes and gambling positions; hedge, PE and VC funds; personal margin and shorts. Commodities come later. FX trading opens only when a country launches its own currency.
- **One currency at start (Logan, batch 124).** Every country uses the US dollar, every exchange lists in dollars, and the Federal Monetary Board sets one world base rate (1.75% at start **(T)**); each country's bonds and loans price at that rate plus a country spread. Other central banks are dormant until their country re-currencies ([detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md) 3.8.1).
- **Information.** Accurate market data comes only from the Pdoomberg terminal ([11-ui.md](11-ui.md) 11.1.1).
