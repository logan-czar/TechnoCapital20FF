# 4. Dynasty and succession

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Full rules: [detailed/1-corporate-core.md](detailed/1-corporate-core.md) 1.11._

- **Dynasty.** A premade dynasty with a family trust; 30+ original rival dynasties.
- **Succession.** Not CK3 heirs. On death the player picks from eligible successors: children, partners, mentees, spouses, and people owed big favors or money; will-named successors are shown first. Minors take over with a guardian until 18. With a non-family successor the player chooses whether the dynasty continues. The player gets that character's share of trusts and Techno-Capital.
- **Marriage** is a dynasty alliance with a prenup.
- **Score.** Endless sandbox with a dynasty score ([detailed/12-setup-and-score.md](detailed/12-setup-and-score.md)).
