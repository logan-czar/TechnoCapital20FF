# 4. Dynasty and succession

## 4.1 Structure

- **Dynasty**: the family name. Earns **renown** and unlocks **legacies** (for example "Old Money": +reputation; "Quant Bloodline": +Finance for children; "Political Machine": +political influence).
- **House**: a branch of the dynasty. Cadet houses can split off with their own trust and become rivals.
- **Family trust**: holds the dynasty's core assets under rules that persist across the playthrough (see [02-trusts-and-assets.md](02-trusts-and-assets.md)). Whoever is trustee controls the dynasty.

## 4.2 Succession laws (rules of the family trust)

| Law | How it works | Tension |
|---|---|---|
| **Primogeniture** | Eldest child becomes trustee | Younger children build personal power bases |
| **Partition** | Trust split into one trust per child | Weakens control, creates rival houses (CK3 gavelkind) |
| **Trustee Appointment** | Independent trustees pick the successor | Trustees can be bribed or turned |
| **Meritocratic** | Highest combined skill among eligible heirs | Heirs compete; more stress and schemes |
| **Designated Heir** | Head names an heir; contestable if unpopular | Flexible, but invites claims |

Eligibility (dynasty blood only, blood plus spouses, anyone adopted into the family) is another trust rule.

## 4.3 What happens on succession

- **Trusteeship** passes by the law; family trust assets stay put.
- **Personal assets** pass by will, which can create stray stakes held by siblings, ex-spouses or charities, and new bloc members.
- Bloc members, boards and vassal CEOs re-evaluate the new head. Low opinion leads to bloc defections and factions. **Succession is the main crisis moment.**
- Debts, margin loans, hooks, favors owed and rivalries are inherited.
- Political offices are not inherited, but the dynasty's political influence persists through its assets.

## 4.4 Marriage and alliances

- **Marriage** allies dynasties and can include a share dowry or a merger clause.
- **Prenups** decide which trust children and shares belong to.
- **Alliances** obligate allies to vote with your bloc in proxy fights and back you in takeovers.
- **Adoption** and **legitimizing** heirs change eligibility.

## 4.5 Game over and scoring

The game ends when no living dynasty member is trustee of the family trust, or at a chosen end date. Score is renown plus total value of the family trust plus personal assets of living members.
