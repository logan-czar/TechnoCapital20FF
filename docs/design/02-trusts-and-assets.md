# 2. Trusts, accounts and assets

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74; batch 116: personal trusts only, the family trust is removed). Full rules: [detailed/1-corporate-core.md](detailed/1-corporate-core.md) (trusts 1.10, personal money 1.10.6); investing: [detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md) 3.0._

- **Landless characters.** Characters hold assets instead of land: company stakes, real estate pools, bonds, funds, accounts. Assets play the role of titles.
- **Personal trusts and accounts.** Replace CK3 gold. The character's money sits in their own personal trusts and accounts, free-form, and the player can edit them at any time. There is no family trust (batch 116); when John Techno dies the game ends, so nothing is inherited.
- **Personal money.** Salary and bonus, dividends, trust payouts and gambling; monthly spending budgets raise resources; personal tax by home country; personal loans, margin, shorts and pledged shares. A public rich list leaves out hidden wealth.
- **Feel.** "Wall Street Raiders Lite".
