_Status: draft for Logan's review. Logan asked to use the existing characters and fill the gaps with new ones._
- _**Existing (6):** John Techno, Terry Tippacanoe, Antone Provolone, Ricky Feng, Dot Percy, and Terry's Montreal MP friend, who is now named. Their premises are Logan's ([7-starting-world.md](../detailed/7-starting-world.md) 7.3). Their age, traits, ideology, skills and holdings were never set, so the ones below are proposals **(A)**._
- _**New (4):** Beau Lacoste, Corinne Vale, Hollis Brandt and Inés Villarreal. They would join the "16 more AI rival characters" in 7.4._

_All characters follow the current rules:_
- _3 personality traits from the 12 pairs (1.1.2)_
- _6 skills_
- _one of the nine ideologies_
- _no family mechanics_
- _no stress_

_Skills and wealth are (T)._

**Wealth by profile:**
- **Rich, with a burden**: $1B to $10B.
- **Connected**: $1M to $50M.
- **Talented**: under $1M.

**Skill order:** Finance / Operations / Persuasion / Intrigue / Tech / Hustle.

## 1. John Techno (Edmonton): the player

**Premise.** John is a 22-year-old former union man from Alberta's oil fields, a local legend, single, with two moms. He is COO of Arrowhead Drilling.

**Starting position.** $100k cash, a $1M salary and options on 2% of Arrowhead.

**Traits.** His only trait is Operational Excellence, which doubles the buffs of the other board members while he sits as COO, CEO or Chair.

**Ideology.** It is left unset so the player shapes it.

## 2. Terry Tippacanoe (Edmonton)

**Premise.** Terry is a casino owner and financier of questionable origins. He plays up being 1/8th Native American as part of his grift.

**Holdings.** He owns 60% of Arrowhead Drilling and a very popular casino.

**Edges.** He gets +25% success and -25% discovery on schemes against Liberal characters, and the media never exposes him.

**Proposed.** Deceitful and Greedy fit the grift, and Gregarious fits a casino host. He is a Chud, which fits his anti-Liberal edge.

## 3. Gilles Thibodeau (Montreal): Terry's MP friend

**Premise.** Gilles is a Conservative MP for a Montreal seat and Terry's Friend, which gives Terry pull in federal politics. He was previously unnamed.

**Proposed.**
- He is a cautious backbencher who trades votes for favors, with a soft spot for Quebec autonomy (Sovereigntists).
- His hook: he owes Terry for paying off his campaign debts.

## 4. Antone Provolone (New York)

**Premise.** Antone is a finance bro by day and a wise guy by night. He always knows a guy who knows a guy, and he has worked his way into DC.

**Proposed.**
- Holdings: a boutique brokerage (Asset management, about $30M) and a stake in a waste-hauling front.
- He is a Liberal donor, which puts him in Terry's crosshairs.
- His DC contact is Hollis Brandt.

## 5. Ricky Feng (Bay Area)

**Premise.** Ricky is a hacker who barely got out of a hack gone bad alive. He holds a very large, diverse crypto wallet he can no longer get into, a very large amount of compute in Asia, and a bot farm SaaS in the Philippines and Thailand.

**Proposed.** He is a Paranoid, Visionary Accelerationist, and he hides from whoever he hacked.

## 6. Dot Percy (Los Angeles)

**Premise.** Dot is the widow of Hollywood's greatest power couple. Her husband died just as she worked up the courage to ask for a divorce. She has the estate and five talentless adult children who embarrass her in the press (flavor only).

**Proposed.**
- Holdings: a film studio and a stake in a streamer (16-media).
- She is a Narcissist who guards her Image, and her five children keep feeding her Heat.

## 7. Beau Lacoste (Houston): new

**Premise.** Beau is a loud Gulf Coast wildcatter worth about $3B in oil and LNG. Oil never crashed in 2014, so he borrowed heavily to expand.

**Burden.** About $2B of reserve-backed loans come due in 2021.

**Vice.** Gambling: he is a regular at Corinne Vale's high-roller tables.

**AI goal.** Survive the debt wall by buying cheap small producers. He wants Arrowhead Drilling, which puts him against Terry and John.

## 8. Corinne Vale (Las Vegas): new

**Premise.** Corinne is the grand dame of the Strip: casinos, convention halls and the biggest sportsbook in Nevada. She calls Terry's casino "a bingo hall with a grift attached".

**Burden.** Her company has skimmed its own books for decades, and the tough accounting law passed after Enron makes the skim ever harder to hide.

**AI goal.** Become the world's leading Gambling company, starting by crushing Terry.

## 9. Hollis Brandt (Washington DC): new

**Premise.** Hollis is a K Street lobbyist. In 2017 and 2018 he brokered the corporate help behind Obama's hunt for the Trump crime family: the data, jets and private investigators.

**Holdings.** A lobbying firm (Professional services, about $40M) and a stack of hooks on senators and executives.

**Secret.** He kept copies of the case files, and they implicate his own corporate clients.

**AI goal.** Place a client in the cabinet after the November 2020 election, whoever wins.

## 10. Inés Villarreal (Monterrey): new

**Premise.** Inés runs a steel and cement group that is riding the nearshoring wave.

**Burden.** Her construction unit has paid a cartel for protection since the drug war, and the cartel now wants her trucks too.

**AI goal.** Become Mexico's biggest industrial group and get free of the cartel, by hiring help or by dealing with Pablo Segundo.

## Ties

- **Arrowhead Drilling:** Terry holds it, Beau wants it, and John runs it.
- **Gambling:** Corinne and Terry are rivals.
- **Montreal:** Gilles is Terry's Friend in Ottawa politics.
- **The US election:** Antone and Hollis both work DC.
- **Pablo Segundo:** Inés is linked to him through the cartel.
