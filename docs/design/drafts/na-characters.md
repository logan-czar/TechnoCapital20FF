# North American characters (draft v3, 2026-10-18)

_Status: draft for Logan's review; nothing here is in [7-starting-world.md](../detailed/7-starting-world.md) yet._

**What every entry has:** age, hub, profile and wealth, appearance, traits, ideology, skills, holdings, premise, secret or burden, AI goal and ties.

**Canon versus proposals:**
- **Canon:** premises from Logan's rulings (7.3, 8-quest-lines).
- **Proposals (A):** names Claude gave to unnamed characters, and every age, appearance, trait, ideology and skill not set before.

**Rules followed:** every character has 3 personality traits from the 12 pairs (1.1.2) and one of the nine ideologies. There are no family mechanics and no stress.

**Skills:** in the order Finance / Operations / Persuasion / Intrigue / Tech / Hustle **(T)**. John Techno's 24s are the best in the game.

**Wealth by profile:**
- **Rich:** $1B to $10B.
- **Connected:** $1M to $50M.
- **Talented:** under $1M.
