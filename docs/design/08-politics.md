# 8. Politics

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74; batch 116: the player character, not a dynasty; batch 121: only national governments; Consistency pass 3 (batch 129): the EU exception added). Full rules: [detailed/4-politics.md](detailed/4-politics.md) and [detailed/5-ideology.md](detailed/5-ideology.md)._

- **Actors.** The nine ideologies (Liberals, Communists, Accelerationists, Effective Altruists, Transhumanists, Neo-Luddites, Sovereigntists, Landites, Chuds) are the political actors; votes are counted by strata.
- **The player character** does not rule; John Techno buys influence over those who do.
- **Only national governments** (batch 121): each country's national government sets the laws for the whole country. There are no state, regional or city governments, councils, mayors or local elections; rezonings, permits and policing contracts are decisions of national ministers. Sub-national governments may come back after the POC. The one body above the nation is the **EU**, a supranational body over its members' national laws ([detailed/4-politics.md](detailed/4-politics.md) 4.25.1); Norway and Iceland follow its single market laws, Switzerland stands alone, and the UK left on 31 December 2019.
- **Laws and taxes.** Corporate tax per country on profit earned there; havens via shells. No antitrust. Countries can license, cap or ban frontier AI training.
- **Politics by company rank** (4.28): what politics offers grows with the player's company rank.
