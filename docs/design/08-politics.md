# 8. Politics

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Full rules: [detailed/4-politics.md](detailed/4-politics.md) and [detailed/5-ideology.md](detailed/5-ideology.md)._

- **Actors.** The nine ideologies (Liberals, Communists, Accelerationists, Effective Altruists, Transhumanists, Neo-Luddites, Sovereigntists, Landites, Chuds) are the political actors; votes are counted by strata.
- **The dynasty** does not rule; it buys influence over those who do.
- **Laws and taxes.** Corporate tax per country on profit earned there; havens via shells. No antitrust. Countries can license, cap or ban frontier AI training.
- **Politics by company rank** (4.28): what politics offers grows with the player's company rank.
