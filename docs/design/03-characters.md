# 3. Characters

## 3.1 Skills

Every character has five skills (0 to 30):

| Skill | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Finance** | Trust returns, valuation, leverage, CFO tasks, trading |
| **Operations** | Company growth and efficiency as CEO or COO, facility output |
| **Persuasion** | Pitches, proxy fights, bloc-building, lobbying, campaigns. Renamed from "Influence" (Logan, 2026-10-17: influence is not a skill; it is the amount of influence you have in an area) |
| **Intrigue** | Schemes, counter-intelligence, uncovering secrets, running shells |
| **Tech** | R&D, cyberware tolerance, netrunning, tech-sector pitches |

## 3.2 Traits

- **Personality** (3 per character, opposed pairs): Ambitious/Content, Greedy/Generous, Paranoid/Trusting, Ruthless/Compassionate, Visionary/Conservative, Honest/Deceitful, Brave/Cautious.
- **Education**: Ivy League MBA, Bootcamp Coder, Military Contractor, Street-Taught, Old Money Tutors, Quant PhD, Law School, Party School (political elite).
- **Lifestyle**: Workaholic, Socialite, Ascetic, Hedonist, Philanthropist.
- **Health and body**: Burnout, Addiction, Chronic Illness, cyberware traits.
- **Notoriety**: Scandal-Ridden, Media Darling, Under Investigation, Blacklisted.
- **Origin**: birthplace and where they were raised (a hub and country). Gives local influence bonuses there (see [05-map-and-influence.md](05-map-and-influence.md)) and shapes culture and language.

## 3.3 Opinion and relationships

Opinion (-100 to 100) is a sum of modifiers: traits, family ties, favors, slights, money from the trust, shared origin. Named relationships: Rival, Friend, Mentor/Protégé, Lover, Nemesis, Business Partner, Patron (political).

**Hooks** (leverage) are held over specific characters from secrets, debts, and favors owed, for example favors owed to a father's friends after an Ask Daddy founding.

## 3.4 Stress

Acting against traits raises stress (a Compassionate CEO ordering mass layoffs). Coping through lifestyle lowers it. High stress triggers breakdowns: burnout, public meltdown, addiction, defection.

## 3.5 Life path

Characters are born, educated, start careers (employee, executive, founder, politician, fixer), marry, age and die. A character's **time** matters in hourly turns: meetings, pitches and travel take hours, so the dynasty head cannot be everywhere at once. Delegating to family and board members is essential.

Careers outside business:
- **Politician**: can be elected or appointed (see [08-politics.md](08-politics.md)), giving the dynasty direct political power.
- **Fixer / netrunner**: runs deniable operations.
- **Media figure**: shapes public opinion and influence campaigns.

## 3.6 Schemes

Run over many turns with a success chance from the relevant skill versus defenses:

- **Hostile**: Dig up dirt (gain a hook), Leak scandal, Corporate espionage, Poach executive, Netrun (steal data, crash systems), Investigate shell, Sabotage facility, Arrange "accident".
- **Personal**: Befriend, Seduce, Recruit to bloc, Mentor, Blackmail, Court patron.
- **Political**: Bribe official, Fund candidate, Astroturf campaign.

Discovery costs opinion and reputation and can start lawsuits, regulatory probes or criminal investigations.

## 3.7 Cyberpunk layer

- **Cyberware**: implants granting skill boosts (neural co-processor +Finance, social-engineering suite +Persuasion, reflex boosters for security staff) at the cost of **humanity**, with failure and rejection events. Legality varies by country.
- **Life extension**: expensive treatments that delay death and frustrate heirs.
- **Private security and PMCs**: bodyguards, corporate security forces, contractors hired for "risk management" abroad.
- **AI assistants**: buy an AI advisor that boosts a skill but can be hacked.
- **Digital consciousness** (late-game, optional): an upload of a dead head that can advise or haunt the dynasty.

## 3.8 Resources

| Resource | Role | CK3 analogue |
|---|---|---|
| **Money and assets** | Trusts and personal accounts | Gold and titles |
| **Influence** | Local power from assets (see map file) | Control and prestige |
| **Reputation** | Legitimacy with markets, regulators and the public | Prestige plus tyranny |
| **Renown** | Dynasty-wide score unlocking legacies | Renown |
| **Hooks** | Leverage over specific characters | Hooks |
| **Time** | Hours available per turn for actions | (new) |
