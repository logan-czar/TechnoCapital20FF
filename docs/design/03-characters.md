# 3. Characters

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Full rules: [detailed/1-corporate-core.md](detailed/1-corporate-core.md) 1.1._

- **Skills.** Six skills from 0 to 30: Finance, Operations, Persuasion, Intrigue, Tech, Hustle. They grow through education, jobs, events and age, peak around 45 to 55, then fade. Influence is not a skill; it is how much you have in an area.
- **Traits.** 3 to 5 traits from the 12 opposed pairs, plus hidden traits. Traits alone drive AI behavior.
- **Relationships.** Friend, Neutral or Rival tags (plus Nemesis and special bonds), not a numeric opinion. Hooks are weak or strong.
- **Ambitions** are for the player only. AI stress is a 0 to 3 level run by the director.
- **Intel and schemes.** Secrets are known or not; CK3 style schemes run for weeks with agents. Hacking is its own system ([detailed/17-hacking.md](detailed/17-hacking.md)).
- **Time.** Most actions are instant; some burn ticks. Delegated actions are rolled by the delegate.
- **Vices.** Gambling, drugs, doomscrolling and sex ([detailed/11-addiction.md](detailed/11-addiction.md)). Cyberware is scrapped.
