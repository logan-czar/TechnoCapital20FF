# 20. User interface: detailed design

_Status: draft v0.1 (2026-10-20). Batch 133 (Logan): CK3 layout (map always behind, top bar, left icon rail opening panels over the map, right-side outliner); Pdoomberg uses the same look as the rest of the UI; right-click context menus on every person, company, province and holding; clickable alert icons along the top plus corner toasts, auto-pause set per alert type; only stories about you, your companies or your holdings pop up; the phone is a large panel (about a third of the screen width, near full height) and a major hub; unknown values show "?" with a hint on how to learn them; every screen laid out here (the clickable HTML mockup is built separately). Expands [../11-ui.md](../11-ui.md), which stays the overview. Builds on [../09-time-and-turns.md](../09-time-and-turns.md) (speeds, auto-pause), [2-map-and-influence.md](2-map-and-influence.md) 2.22 and 2.24, [3-markets-and-fx.md](3-markets-and-fx.md) 3.13 and 3.14, [4-politics.md](4-politics.md) 4.15, [13-events.md](13-events.md) 13.2.1, [9-gambling.md](9-gambling.md), [11-addiction.md](11-addiction.md), [8-quest-lines.md](8-quest-lines.md) 8.2, [12-setup-and-score.md](12-setup-and-score.md), [16-media.md](16-media.md) and [1-corporate-core.md](1-corporate-core.md). ## 20.1 Principles

| Principle | What it means on screen |
|---|---|
| **The map is home** | The world map is always behind everything. Panels, the phone and the outliner slide over it; nothing replaces it except the event modal and the main menu. |
| **Sources, not god screens** | No all-knowing info screen ([../11-ui.md](../11-ui.md) 11.1.1). Your own things are exact; everything about others comes from News (biased), Pdoomberg (accurate, published), Intel (accurate, what you know) or Search (what was published). |
| **"?" is honest** | A value you do not know shows **?**. Its tooltip says how to learn it (20.3.4). A learned value shows the date it was learned. |
| **Every number explains itself** | CK3 nested tooltips: hover any number for its breakdown, and any term inside the tooltip for its own (20.3.2). |
| **One look** | One flat neon-on-dark style everywhere (11.4 palette), **Pdoomberg included** (batch 133). In-world products (Pdoomberg, the phone's apps, outlets) carry their own name and logo, not their own style. |
| **Act from anywhere** | Right-click any person, company, province, holding, outlet or politician for its actions; panels also carry action buttons (20.3.1). |
| **Pause only for what matters** | Events always pause. Alerts pause only if their type is set to Pause (20.4). Everything else waits in the alert row, the outliner, the phone or the News feed. |
| **The phone is a place** | A large panel with apps, not a menu. Gambling, crypto, shopping, texts, calls, the feed and travel live there (20.6). The game keeps running while it is open. |
| **Costs before clicks** | Every action shows its cost, ticks and the skill it rolls before it is sent; odds are never shown (13.2). Orders show the full preview (3.13). |

- **Toasts** stack upward in the bottom right of the map area, just left of the outliner, and fade after 6 seconds **(T)**.

### 20.2.3 The outliner (right side)

A narrow column (about 15% of the width **(T)**), collapsible with Tab. Sections fold open and closed and can be reordered.

| Section | Holds | Click / right-click |
|---|---|---|
| **Quests** | Tracked quest steps with the next goal and any deadline | Opens the quest log at that step |
| **Active actions** | Ticking actions with progress bars (travel, meetings, deliveries, schemes with weekly progress, scroll sessions) **(A)** | Opens the action; right-click to cancel |
| **Pinned people** | Portrait, name, tag (Friend, Neutral, Rival), a busy marker, health warning | Character panel; context menu |
| **Pinned companies** | Name, price and day change (needs Pdoomberg), who controls it | Company panel; context menu |
| **Watchlist** | Pdoomberg watchlist rows (3.13.1), live with a subscription, delayed without (20.5.2) | Opens the Pdoomberg quote |
| **Alerts** | The same alerts as the top row, as a readable list with one line each | Opens the alert's target |

Anything can be pinned from its panel header or its context menu.

### 20.2.4 Panels: opening, stacking, closing (A)

- A rail icon or a link opens a panel in **slot 1**, sliding in from the left edge.
- A link clicked inside a panel **replaces** it, with **back and forward** buttons and a breadcrumb (Arrowhead > Board > Terry). **Shift+click** opens the link in **slot 2** beside it instead.
- At most **two** panels are open. Opening a third replaces the unpinned one that was opened first; a **pinned** panel stays put.
- **Wide panels** (Pdoomberg, Politics, Holdings tree) take both slots; they can be narrowed to one.
- **Esc** closes the last panel opened; with nothing open it opens the main menu. Clicking empty map does not close panels.
- Panels never pause the game. The game keeps running behind them at the chosen speed.

### 20.2.5 The phone slot

- The phone sits as a **handle** at the bottom right (a dark slab with the clock and badge counts). Click it or press **P** to slide the phone up: about **a third of the screen width, near full height** (Logan, batch 133), anchored bottom right.
- While it is open the outliner folds into a thin icon strip, and panel slot 2 narrows so the map stays visible between the panels and the phone **(A)**.
- **The game keeps running** while the phone is open. P or Esc slides it down; its app keeps its place.
- The event modal covers the phone; the phone returns when the event closes.

## 20.3 Interaction model

### 20.3.1 Right-click context menus (CK3 interactions)

Right-click any object, on the map, in a panel, in a list or in the outliner, for its menu. Each item shows its **skill icon** if it rolls, its **cost**, its **ticks** if it is not instant (1.1.6) and a **lock reason** when greyed out ("Needs a hook", "Not in the same city: remote call at -30%", "Needs a Pdoomberg subscription"). Odds are never shown (13.2). Top items: **Open**, **Pin**, **Search**. The most used items are also buttons on the object's panel.

| Object | Example actions (source) |
|---|---|
| **Person** | Meet (2 ticks, or a remote call at -30%, 2.21.1) · Call or Message (opens the phone) · Gift · Befriend · Seduce · Dig up dirt · Investigate · Leak scandal · Blackmail (needs a hook) · Call in a hook · Destroy evidence · Poach executive · Offer a board seat · Propose or recruit to a bloc (1.4) · Bring their vice to the table (in a negotiation, 11.6) · Hack their devices (needs Techno-Capital, 17.2) · Arrange "accident" (1.1.8) · Point a rival rotation character at them (7.4) |
| **Company** | Open on Pdoomberg · Buy or sell shares (order ticket) · Tender offer (1.9.3) · Propose a bloc to a holder · Investigate shell (if "Undisclosed holder") · Corporate espionage · Market research (2.22.2) · Hack its servers (17.2) · Buy a media campaign for or against it (16.4) · Offer a software licence (14.4) · Add to watchlist. Own companies add: set growth plan and R&D, call a board meeting, takeover defenses (1.9.4), merge or keep a fully owned target |
| **Province** | Open province card · Buy or sell pool shares (2.16) · Develop land · Rezone (a Win a decision objective, 4.15.2) · Travel here (opens the phone's Travel app) · Market research here ($100k a month, 2.22.2) · Found a company with its HQ here · Show influence breakdown |
| **Holding** (a stake, pool share, business unit, jet, compute) | Open · Sell · Pledge for a loan (1.10.6) · Move to another of your holders (trust, shell) · Put in a figurehead's name (1.3.1) · Units: set growth plan, R&D, delegate to the CPU (8.2.3) · Compute: lease out or end a lease · Jet: plan an itinerary |
| **Outlet** | Open outlet (audience, slant; credibility hidden, 16.5) · Buy a campaign on it · Plant a story (needs ownership or a hook on the editor, 3.14.4) · Sue over a story · Search its stories · Go to its owner. Owned outlets add: set slant, run, soften or kill a story, point an investigation at a target (16.5.1) |
| **Politician** | Meet · Call in a favor · Donate (within G1 caps) · Bribe · Blackmail · Lobby on their bill · Back or endorse (4.15.1 Elections) · Request a decision or Protection (ministers and agency heads, 4.15.2) · Hire as Political Director (lobbyists and consultants) |

### 20.3.2 Tooltips

- **Every number** has a breakdown tooltip: base, each modifier with its source, the total ("Favor +2: base 0, union legend +5, Liberal press -3").
- **Nested (CK3 style)**: underlined terms inside a tooltip open their own tooltip. A tooltip **locks** after 0.5 seconds of hover or while Alt is held **(T)**, so the player can move into it.
- Learned values add a line: "Learned 3 Mar 2020 by Investigate (may be out of date)" (2.22.2).

### 20.3.3 Hover cards

Hovering a **name** (person, company, outlet, province) for 0.3 seconds **(T)** shows a small card without opening a panel:
- Person: portrait, age, role, location, tag and bond, known traits, Clout and Fame, any hook either way.
- Company: logo, rank, industry, price and day change, owner of record, real controller (or ?).
- Outlet: kind, owner, slant badge, audience size band.
- Province: country, development, city rank, John's influence tier there.

### 20.3.4 Unknown values ("?")

- Any field the player does not know shows **?** (Logan, batch 133). Its tooltip lists the ways to learn it, with costs:

| Unknown | Hint shown |
|---|---|
| Hidden traits | "? more traits. Meet them, or Dig up dirt or Investigate (Intrigue, $50k to $100k, 1.1.8)" |
| Real controller | "Real controller: ?. Investigate the holder or the shell, place an informant, or wait for an exposé (1.3.1)" |
| Private company financials | "? Private company. One secret per intel action: market research, a research subscription, espionage (2.22.2, 3.14.2)" |
| Published figures without Pdoomberg | "? Published on Pdoomberg. Subscribe ($25k a year), or read the delayed news (20.5.2)" |
| Rival influence share | "Kingmaker (exact share ?). An intel action learns it (2.22.1)" |
| Vril | Never shown unless John has Vril Touch; no "?" (1.1.0) |

- Estimates show a band, not a "?": estimated fair value as "$14 (12 to 16)" (3.14.2), rivals' scores as "~1,200" (12.4).

## 20.4 Alerts and notifications

### 20.4.1 The alert row

Alert icons sit along the top bar, **sorted by urgency** (Pause types first). Each icon has a count and a tooltip with one line per instance. Click opens the target; right-click offers **Dismiss**, **Snooze 1 day** and **Settings for this type**. Every type has a setting: **Pause**, **Notify** (icon and toast), **Log** (event log only) or **Off**; the few marked locked cannot drop below Pause (3.13.5) **(A: one ladder for 9.1, 3.13.5 and 4.15.3)**.

| Icon | Alert | Trigger | Default | Clears when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ! | **Event waiting** | Any event for John (13.2) | Pause (locked) | Answered |
| Q | **Quest step** | A new or due quest step | Notify | Step opened |
| $! | **Margin call** | Margin warning or call, forced sale (3.13.5) | Pause (locked) | Margin restored |
| Flag | **Disclosure filing** | A holder crosses a line in a company John controls or holds 5%+ of | Pause (locked) | Opened |
| Sword | **Takeover bid** | A bid or tender for such a company (1.9.3) | Pause (locked) | Offer ends |
| Heart | **Health** | John's health event or health under 30 **(T)** | Pause | Opened |
| Cross | **Death** | A character close to John dies (Friend, board, pinned) | Pause | Opened |
| Chart | **Earnings day** | 00:00 on 2 Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct, one per holding (3.1.0) | Pause | Opened, or next day |
| Calendar | **Quarterly recap** | 7 days before earnings, per company where John is an executive or director | Pause | Recap read |
| Crash | **Market crash** | Circuit breaker on an exchange with 10%+ of the portfolio; the crash (8.5) | Pause (locked) | Opened |
| Bomb | **War or coup** | In a country with John's assets (4.15.3) | Pause | Opened |
| Mask | **Exposed** | Hidden control exposed, in either direction (1.3.1) | Pause | Event answered |
| Spy | **Scheme found** | A scheme against John uncovered, or his own discovered (1.1.8) | Pause | Opened |
| Gavel | **Investigation** | Opens, reaches Subpoena or Charges (4.15.3) | Pause | Stage passes |

| Bill | **Bill at stake** | A bill above the Stakes threshold enters Debate or a pass roll (4.15.1) | Pause | Bill resolves |
| Ballot | **Election night** | John or a backed candidate on the ballot | Pause | Night ends |
| Crown | **Vassal unrest** | A vassal turns Restless or Defiant; a faction ultimatum (1.7.3, 1.9.1) | Notify | Loyalty back, or ultimatum answered |
| Chair | **Empty task** | A board seat John controls has no task, or a Director has no budget **(A)** | Notify | Fixed |
| Check | **Action done** | A ticking action, order or delivery finished | Notify | Opened, or after 1 day |
| Lock | **Hack** | A ransom lock or backdoor found on John's systems (17.3) | Pause | Resolved |
| Star | **Milestone** | Score milestone (12.4) | Notify | Opened |

| Phone | **Phone** | Unread messages, missed calls (20.6.3) | Notify (badge on the handle) | Read |

### 20.4.2 Toasts

- Small cards, bottom right of the map area: an icon, one line and a link. They never pause.
- They carry Notify alerts, finished actions, fills and small price moves. While the game runs fast they are **batched** into one card ("While you slept: 14 fills, 3 alerts, portfolio +1.2%", 3.13.5).
- The **morning brief** (07:00, 3.13.6) and the **weekly political brief** (Monday 07:00, 4.15.5) arrive as toasts that open a one-page card, and also on the phone (20.6.3) **(A)**.

- **Left (about 60%)**: header (deck icon and colour, scope, date), portraits, scenario text, and **the decisions at the bottom** (13.2.1). Each option: skill icon, costs, ticks, suited trait. No odds, no timer.

## 20.5 The information sources

### 20.5.1 News feed

A one-slot panel (also the phone's News app). A scrolling list of story cards, newest first.

```
+----------------------------------------------------+
| NEWS   [About me] [My companies] [Holdings] [All]  |
| Filters: industry v  country v  outlet v  slant v  |
+----------------------------------------------------+
| [Liberal] THE ATLANTIC LEDGER - Major paper   2h   |
| "Arrowhead names union legend to its board"        |
| Arrowhead (ARWD)   John Techno     [Search] [...]  |
+----------------------------------------------------+
| [Chud] TRUTH CRUSADE (creator) - Social      5h    |
| "Edmonton oil kid is a plant, sources say"  RUMOR  |
+----------------------------------------------------+
```

- **Every card names its outlet** and shows its **slant badge** (the ideology icon and colour, or "Neutral"), the outlet's tier (Wire, Major paper, Trade, Tabloid and TV, Forums and social, Anonymous leak; 3.14.4) and the time. Credibility stays hidden (16.5) **(A: the tier shows, the reliability % does not)**.
- **Tabs**: About me, My companies, Holdings, Watchlist, All. **Filters**: industry, country, outlet, slant, kind (report, scandal, rumor, opinion), outlet tier.
- **Pop-up rule (Logan, batch 133)**: only stories **about John, his companies or his holdings** pop up, as a news card toast with the alert "Story about you" (20.4.1). Everything else, including AI characters' event results (13.4), goes only to the feed.
- Rumors carry a RUMOR tag; right-click offers "Check this story" (an intel action that learns its truth as one secret, 3.14.4).
- Owned outlets show a small key icon: the owner's run, soften and kill choices are made from the outlet panel (16.5).
- There is no ticker (11.4).

### 20.5.2 Pdoomberg

A wide panel (both slots) in the main UI style, with the Pdoomberg name and logo in its header and an "Accurate: published data" badge. It never shows what companies hide (11.1.1).

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

| search ticker or name ______   account: John (personal) v    sub: active until 1 Jan 2021    |
+--------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| WATCHLIST: My holdings v       |  ARWD  Arrowhead Drilling   $ --.--  closed (1 Jan)         |
| ARWD   --.--   --    EFV ..    |  [chart: hourly / daily / weekly, EFV band, events]         |
| ...                            |  Holders over the first line: Tippacanoe 60% ...            |
| CALENDAR: 2 Jan earnings       |  Next: earnings 2 Jan 00:00   Analysts: 0 (small cap)      |
+--------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

| Tab | Shows (3.13.1, 3.14) |
|---|---|
| **Quotes** | Watchlists (built-in: My holdings, My companies, Rivals' companies, Disclosure radar), market overview (GRG, indices, Retail Fever, cycle phase by country, yield curves, Funding Stress Index), the calendar |
| **Chart** | Hourly, daily and weekly bars; overlays: estimated fair value band, index, event markers, volume, short interest |
| **Order** | Order ticket with the full preview (fill price, slippage, impact, fees, tax, margin, disclosure lines crossed); all order types (3.13.3); account picker (3.13.2); positions and open orders below |
| **Filings** | Quarterly statements, guidance, disclosure filings with filer names (shells show the shell's name), annual meeting notices |

- **Subscription rule**: $25k a year **(T)**, per character or per company (11.1.1). John starts with one paid by Arrowhead **(A)**.
- **Without a subscription**: prices show **delayed one day (T)** with a "Delayed" badge; Filings, Ratings, Industries and Screener show **?** with the subscribe hint (20.3.4). The Order tab still works through a retail broker, with the ticket marked "Delayed quote" **(A)**.
- Prices are exact and live with a subscription; there are no FX rates in the POC (batch 125).

### 20.5.3 Intel

A one-slot panel of everything John knows for certain (11.1.1), plus his running intel work.

| Tab | Lists |
|---|---|
| **Secrets** | Every secret John holds, by target: hidden traits, affairs, crimes, addictions, hidden owners, real controllers, plans, flight logs (1.1.8.2). Hooks made from them are marked |
| **People** | Characters with at least one known secret: what is known, what is still ? |
| **Companies** | Real controllers and hidden holders found; learned private financials |
| **Rumors** | Unchecked rumors from the feed, texts and doomscrolling, with source and tier; checked ones marked True or False |
| **Operations** | John's running schemes, hacks, market research and informants, with weekly progress and discovery risk shown as a band **(A)** |

- **Confidence** per entry: **Certain** (a secret; it stays true until the target changes it), **Dated** (a figure; shows "as of" its date, 2.22.2), **Rumor** (unchecked; shows the source tier).
- **Source** per entry: the scheme, informant, hack, event, research subscription, board seat or person who told John.
- Right-click an entry: use as a hook, leak it, trade it, or check a rumor.

### 20.5.4 Search

A one-slot panel (also the phone's Search app). Neutral: nobody can bury or boost results (11.1.1).

- **Query** a person, company, outlet, place or topic. Results are **grouped by outlet or creator**, each group headed by its name, kind and **slant badge**, with its items below (article, post, video, campaign, show), date and reach band.
- **Filters**: kind, date range, outlet tier, slant. **Sort**: newest, or most reach.
- **Bias labels**: the slant badge on every group, and a summary bar on top ("12 Liberal, 4 Chud, 3 Neutral sources").
- A paid campaign shows as paid only once it has been exposed (16.4) **(A)**.
- Right-click a group: open the outlet, or follow it (its stories reach the News feed's Watchlist tab).

## 20.6 The phone

### 20.6.1 Size and behavior

- About **a third of the screen width, near full height**, sliding up from the **bottom right** over the map (Logan, batch 133). The map stays visible; the game keeps running.
- It is **John's phone** in the world: a status bar with the in-game time, apps on a home screen, a notification shade.
- Apps open inside the phone. A few hand off to a full panel where a big screen is needed ("Open in Pdoomberg", "Open quest log").
