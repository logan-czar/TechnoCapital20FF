# 19. History: the world from the 1990s to 2020 (detailed design)

_Status: draft v0.1 (2026-10-18). Batch 93: Logan asked to build the game world's history **year by year**, from the 1990s up to the 2020 start. Each year Claude drafts what happened in real life, region by region, and Logan says what happened in the game world and what did not. Sports are assumed to have gone exactly as in real life unless something very notable changes. Builds on [7-starting-world.md](7-starting-world.md) 7.2.0 (the alternate history). Owner: Logan._

**How to read this file.** Everything below is **real history**, as a base. Rows Logan has confirmed or changed are marked **(Logan)**; everything else is a draft for him to rule on. Where a real event clashes with the alternate history already decided (7.2.0), the clash is flagged.

**Regions.** Nine, matching the map: North America; Latin America; Europe; Russia and the former USSR; the Middle East and North Africa; Sub-Saharan Africa; South Asia; East Asia; Southeast Asia and Oceania.

## 19.1 The 1990s: a political summary by region

| Region | The 1990s |
|---|---|
| **North America** | **US:** the Cold War ends and the US is the lone superpower. Bush Sr. wins the 1991 Gulf War but loses in 1992 to Bill Clinton. NAFTA (1994), the Republican sweep of Congress (1994), welfare reform, budget surpluses by 1998, and a long tech-driven boom. Clinton is impeached over the Lewinsky affair (1998) and acquitted (1999). **Canada:** Jean Chrétien's Liberals rule from 1993 and cut the deficit hard; Quebec's 1995 independence referendum fails by just 50.6% to 49.4%. **Mexico:** NAFTA, the Zapatista uprising (1994), the peso crisis and US bailout (1994 to 1995), and the PRI's 70-year grip starts to slip. |
| **Latin America** | Democracy returns almost everywhere, with free-market reforms and privatization (the "Washington Consensus"). Brazil tames hyperinflation with the Real Plan (1994) under Cardoso. Argentina pegs the peso to the dollar under Menem, building toward a crash. Fujimori shuts Peru's congress in a self-coup (1992) and crushes the Shining Path. In Colombia, Pablo Escobar is killed (1993), but the cartels splinter and the FARC grows. Hugo Chávez is elected in Venezuela (1998) on an anti-establishment wave. |
| **Europe** | Germany reunifies (1990). The Maastricht Treaty creates the European Union (1992), and the euro launches on paper in 1999. Yugoslavia breaks apart in war: Bosnia (1992 to 1995, Srebrenica, the Dayton accords) and Kosovo, ending in NATO's bombing of Serbia (1999). NATO takes in Poland, Hungary and the Czech Republic (1999). A "Third Way" centre-left wins across the continent: Blair (1997), Jospin (1997), Schröder (1998). Northern Ireland's Good Friday Agreement (1998). |
| **Russia and the former USSR** | The Soviet Union collapses (1991). Under Yeltsin, shock therapy and rigged privatizations ("loans for shares") create the oligarchs while living standards crash. Yeltsin shells his own parliament (1993), loses the first Chechen war (1994 to 1996), barely wins re-election (1996), and Russia defaults on its debt (1998). A second Chechen war starts in 1999 under a new prime minister, Vladimir Putin, who becomes acting president when Yeltsin resigns on 31 December 1999. Ukraine and the other republics become independent and poor. |
| **Middle East and North Africa** | The US-led coalition drives Iraq out of Kuwait (1991); Iraq lives under sanctions and no-fly zones, bombed again in Operation Desert Fox (1998). The Oslo Accords (1993) raise hopes of Israeli-Palestinian peace until Rabin's assassination (1995). Algeria's civil war kills over 100,000. Iran's reformist Khatami wins (1997). Al-Qaeda bombs US embassies in Kenya and Tanzania (1998). Gulf oil money builds Dubai into a hub. |
| **Sub-Saharan Africa** | Apartheid ends and Nelson Mandela is elected (1994). The Rwandan genocide (1994) spills into the Congo wars, the second of which (from 1998) draws in eight countries. Civil wars in Sierra Leone, Liberia and Somalia. Nigeria suffers Abacha's dictatorship until his death (1998) and returns to civilian rule under Obasanjo (1999). Ethiopia and Eritrea go to war (1998). HIV/AIDS spreads across the south and east. |
| **South Asia** | India opens its economy (1991, Rao and Manmohan Singh) and Bangalore's IT industry takes off. Hindu nationalism rises with the BJP (the Babri Masjid demolition, 1992). India and Pakistan both test nuclear weapons (1998) and fight the Kargil war (1999); Musharraf seizes power in Pakistan (1999). The Taliban take Kabul (1996) and host Osama bin Laden. |
| **East Asia** | Japan's bubble bursts (1990 to 1991) and the "Lost Decade" begins. China grows fast after Deng's Southern Tour (1992) and gets Hong Kong back (1997) and Macau (1999). The Taiwan Strait crisis (1995 to 1996). South Korea is hit hard by the 1997 Asian financial crisis and an IMF bailout. North Korea loses Kim Il-sung (1994), suffers famine, and freezes its nuclear program under a deal with the US (1994). |
| **Southeast Asia and Oceania** | The "Tiger" economies boom until the 1997 Asian financial crisis starts in Thailand. Suharto falls in Indonesia (1998). East Timor votes for independence (1999) amid militia violence, and an Australian-led force restores order. Malaysia's Mahathir defies the IMF with capital controls. Australia turns conservative under John Howard (1996). |

## 19.2 The year 2000

**Global (draft).** **Y2K** passes on 1 January with almost no failures. The **dot-com bubble peaks**: the NASDAQ tops out on 10 March 2000. The **Human Genome Project** announces its first draft (June). The first crew moves into the **International Space Station** (November).

> **Clash with the alternate history.** In real life the dot-com crash **began in March 2000**, before the 2001 divergence. 7.2.0 says the dot-com crash **never happened**. Does 2000 end with the bubble still inflating, or does the divergence start here?

| Region | Big events of 2000 (real) |
|---|---|
| **North America** | 1. **Bush v. Gore**: the US presidential election comes down to Florida and is settled by the Supreme Court in December; George W. Bush wins. 2. **Vicente Fox** wins Mexico's presidency, ending 71 years of PRI rule. 3. **Chrétien** wins a third majority in Canada (November). |
| **Latin America** | 1. **Fujimori falls**: a bribery video of his spy chief Montesinos breaks; Fujimori flees to Japan and resigns by fax (November). 2. **Plan Colombia**: the US approves about $1.3B in anti-drug and military aid (July). 3. **Chávez** is re-elected under his new constitution (July). |
| **Europe** | 1. **Milošević is overthrown** in Serbia's "Bulldozer Revolution" (October). 2. The **euro sinks** to a record low of about $0.83 (October), and the EU agrees the **Treaty of Nice** (December). 3. **Concorde crashes** in Paris (July), the beginning of the end for supersonic travel. |
| **Russia and the former USSR** | 1. **Putin is elected president** (March). 2. The **Kursk** submarine sinks with all 118 crew (August), and Putin's slow response is a scandal. 3. Putin starts taming the oligarchs and their media: **Gusinsky** of NTV is arrested (June). In Ukraine, the journalist **Gongadze** is murdered (September), which turns into a scandal around President Kuchma. |
| **Middle East and North Africa** | 1. The **Camp David summit** fails (July), and the **Second Intifada** begins (September). 2. **Israel withdraws from southern Lebanon** (May). 3. **Hafez al-Assad dies** and his son **Bashar** takes over Syria (June). Also: al-Qaeda bombs the **USS Cole** in Aden (October), which is worth ruling on, since 9/11 never happens in the game. |
| **Sub-Saharan Africa** | 1. **Zimbabwe's land seizures**: Mugabe's government backs the invasion of white-owned farms. 2. Ethiopia and Eritrea sign the **Algiers peace agreement** (December). 3. **Sharia law** spreads across northern Nigerian states, starting in Zamfara, with deadly riots in Kaduna. Also: British troops intervene in **Sierra Leone** (May). |
| **South Asia** | 1. **Bill Clinton visits India** (March), the first US presidential visit in over 20 years. 2. **India's population passes one billion** (May). 3. **The IT outsourcing boom**: Y2K work makes Bangalore and Hyderabad famous, and Infosys and Wipro become global names. |
| **East Asia** | 1. The **first inter-Korean summit**: Kim Dae-jung meets Kim Jong-il in Pyongyang (June) and later wins the Nobel Peace Prize. 2. **Chen Shui-bian** wins in Taiwan, ending more than 50 years of KMT rule (March). 3. **The PlayStation 2 launches** in Japan (March) and becomes the best-selling console ever. Also: the US grants China permanent normal trade relations (October), opening the road to the WTO. |
| **Southeast Asia and Oceania** | 1. The **Sydney Olympics** (September). 2. **Estrada's impeachment** trial in the Philippines (December), which leads to his fall in January 2001. 3. The **ILOVEYOU** computer worm, released from Manila, infects tens of millions of computers worldwide (May). Also: a **coup in Fiji** (May). |
