# 1. Companies and corporate feudalism

_Summary (consistency pass, batch 74). Batch 117: no Ask Daddy founding path. Full rules: [detailed/1-corporate-core.md](detailed/1-corporate-core.md); business units and financing: [detailed/10-industry-economy.md](detailed/10-industry-economy.md) 10.0._

- **Founding.** No founder charter. The Found Company action runs an event chain by founding path: VC, own cash, loan, freestyle, or government connections through influence.
- **Charter and head.** Every company has a corporate charter that sets its head, usually a President and CEO who holds a voting majority or leads the largest bloc. A bloc's leader is its largest shareholder.
- **Two layers of control.** Wall Street Raider style share ownership decides the owner of record, which is public. Who really calls the shots comes from corporate feudalism (vassal chains, blocs, hooks, loyalty, figureheads) and needs intel to see. Hidden control is legal, but exposure can cost the board (1.3.1).
- **Vassals.** Companies hold vassals: subsidiaries (separate corporations that follow parent directives) and shell companies (empty companies that move money and can hold software, compute and data).
- **Board.** Works like the CK3 council; seat holders set the management score.
- **Takeovers.** Tender offers and majority stakes; defenses (poison pill, white knight, buyback, golden parachute) are board votes. On full ownership the player merges or keeps a subsidiary. No antitrust.
- **Company ranks.** Venture, Company, Corporation, Megacorp, Conglomerate by value (1.7.5).
