# 11. User interface (Godot, lightweight 2D)

## 11.1 Principles

- Lightweight 2D in Godot (batch 41; was Phaser): flat panels, readable tables, a stylized vector map. No 3D.
- Every number explains itself: hovering a value shows its breakdown (CK3 style tooltips).
- Notifications and an event log, with filters, keep hourly turns readable.

## 11.1.1 Information comes from sources, not info screens (Logan, batch 67)

> **There is no traditional all-knowing info screen.** The player learns about the world the way a real tycoon does: from sources, each with its own bias and accuracy. This overrides any screen in 11.2 that would show the world's true state for free.

| Source | What it gives | Accurate? |
|---|---|---|
| **The news** | Stories from named outlets ([detailed/16-media.md](detailed/16-media.md)). Every news pop-up and article **names the outlet that ran it**, and carries that outlet's **slant and bias**. Even the news the character reads in game is biased | **No**: slanted, sometimes wrong, sometimes planted |
| **Pdoomberg terminal** | **Stock quotes, charts, order entry and accurate published financial info**: statements (10.0.8), filings, ratings, yields, FX, industry data. The Markets screen lives here | **Yes**, for anything published. It cannot show what companies hide |
| **Intel screen** | **Accurate info** the character has actually learned: secrets (1.1.8.2), traits, real controllers (1.3.1), hidden holdings | **Yes**, but only what you know |
| **Search engine** | **Search any person, company or topic** to see the content created about it: articles, posts, creator videos, campaigns, with the outlet or creator named | Mixed: you see what was published, bias and all |

- The player's **own** holdings, companies and characters are shown accurately on their own screens (Portfolio, Company, Character).
- Anything about **others** comes through the four sources above. A rival's company screen shows published numbers from the terminal and known secrets from intel, and leaves the rest blank or "unknown".
- Owning outlets matters because **owning the news shapes what the world, and AI characters, believe** **(A)**: AI characters also read the news and react to slanted stories.
- **Pdoomberg access (A):** a terminal subscription per character or company ($25k a year) **(T)**; without it the player sees only delayed prices from the free news.

## 11.3 Map rendering

- World map from simplified vector country and region shapes (public domain data such as Natural Earth), drawn as Godot 2D graphics.
- Hubs are nodes with district grids at hub zoom.
- Trade routes drawn as lines with animated flow; chokepoints highlighted.

## 11.4 Look and feel (Logan, 2026-10-17)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Art style | Flat vector UI with a **neon-on-dark cyberpunk palette** and illustrated character portraits. |
| Portraits | **Layered 2D portraits built from parts** (face, hair, clothes, accessories), which age with the character and change with cyberware (visible implants, ocular glow, chrome). Health, stress and addiction show subtly (tired eyes, weight change) **(A)**. |
| Main screen | **The world map**, with panels sliding over it, like Crusader Kings. The Portfolio, Company, Character and other screens in 11.2 open as panels over the map. |
| News | **Changed (Logan, batch 67): news comes from named, biased outlets**, through pop-ups and the News screen (11.1.1). Earlier rule: **event pop-ups only**: news that matters arrives as event cards and alerts (auto-pause rules in [09-time-and-turns.md](09-time-and-turns.md) 9.1). There is no scrolling news ticker. The phone tab's feed stays only as the doomscrolling vice and its rumors ([detailed/11-addiction.md](detailed/11-addiction.md)) **(A)**. |
| Sound | **Minimal or none** for now. |

**Palette (A).** Near-black background (#0B0D12), panel grey (#161A22), text off-white (#E6E8EE), with neon accents used for meaning: cyan for money and gains, magenta for alerts and losses, amber for warnings, green for health and growth. Contrast meets WCAG AA for all text.
