# 11. User interface (Godot, lightweight 2D)

_Batch 117: no stress (portraits show health and addiction only). Batch 116: hourly ticks, not turns; one player character (no Dynasty screen; a Score screen instead); personal trusts only; 42 industries; Clout, Fame and Favor in the top bar._

## 11.1 Principles

- Lightweight 2D in Godot (batch 41; was Phaser): flat panels, readable tables, a stylized vector map. No 3D.
- Every number explains itself: hovering a value shows its breakdown (CK3 style tooltips).
- Notifications and an event log, with filters, keep hourly ticks readable.

## 11.1.1 Information comes from sources, not info screens (Logan, batch 67)

> **There is no traditional all-knowing info screen.** The player learns about the world the way a real tycoon does: from sources, each with its own bias and accuracy. This overrides any screen in 11.2 that would show the world's true state for free.

| Source | What it gives | Accurate? |
|---|---|---|
| **The news** | Stories from named outlets ([detailed/16-media.md](detailed/16-media.md)). Every news pop-up and article **names the outlet that ran it**, and carries that outlet's **slant and bias**. Even the news the character reads in game is biased | **No**: slanted, sometimes wrong, sometimes planted |
| **Pdoomberg terminal** | **Stock quotes, charts, order entry and accurate published financial info**: statements (10.0.8), filings, ratings, yields, FX, industry data. The Markets screen lives here | **Yes**, for anything published. It cannot show what companies hide |
| **Intel screen** | **Accurate info** the character has actually learned: secrets (1.1.8.2), traits, real controllers (1.3.1), hidden holdings | **Yes**, but only what you know |
| **Search engine** | **Search any person, company or topic** to see the content created about it: articles, posts, creator videos, campaigns, with the outlet or creator named | Mixed: you see what was published, bias and all |

- The player's **own** holdings, companies and characters are shown accurately on their own screens (Portfolio, Company, Character).
- Anything about **others** comes through the four sources above. A rival's company screen shows published numbers from the terminal and known secrets from intel, and leaves the rest blank or "unknown".
- **AI characters do not read the news** (Logan, batch 68). Instead, the **director** (detailed/13-events.md 13.3) **may direct AI characters** when a story comes out **about them or characters close to them** (relatives, friends, their companies): a smear can push a rival into a lawsuit, a counter-campaign or a public outburst (a gaffe that adds Heat); a puff piece can make them bolder. Owning outlets matters through Image, ideology, investor confidence and scandals (16), and through these director reactions.
- **False stories** (Logan, batch 68): outlets can publish planted or false stories. If one is **debunked** later (by another outlet's investigation, the target's lawsuit, or an event), the outlet that ran it loses credibility (16.5) and the planter risks discovery as a scheme (1.1.8).
- **The search engine and Pdoomberg are neutral tools** (Logan, batch 68): not owned by anyone in the game, they cannot be bought, and no one can bury or boost results. The search engine shows what was published, bias and all; Pdoomberg shows published data accurately.
- **Pdoomberg access (A):** a terminal subscription per character or company ($25k a year) **(T)**; without it the player sees only delayed prices from the free news.

## 11.2 Main screens

| Screen | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Map** | World, country and hub zoom levels; overlays for influence, ownership, real estate, politics, unrest |
| **Portfolio** | **One Portfolio screen per holder** (Logan, batch 58): each of John's personal trusts, his personal account and each company has its own, switched by a holder picker. Each shows stocks, bonds, real estate pool holdings, private stakes and fund stakes, with cash, cash flow, loans, margin, buy and sell. Crypto lives in the phone-tab gambling system (9). The Wall Street Raiders Lite screen |
| **Personal accounts** | John's own accounts, separate from his trusts |
| **Holdings tree** | Companies John controls (owner of record and, as far as intel shows, real controller, 1.3.1) and their vassals, by vassal type and loyalty |
| **Company** | Charter, head, cap table with blocs, board seats and tasks, business units, financials, vassal contracts, actions |
| **Found company** | Wizard: name, industry, home hub, capital target, founding path, then hands off to the event chain |
| **Character** | Portrait, skills, traits, relationship tags, assets, schedule, actions |
| **Score** | Replaces the Dynasty screen (batch 116): the player's score, milestones, history log and the rival leaderboard ([detailed/12-setup-and-score.md](detailed/12-setup-and-score.md) 12.4) |
| **Pdoomberg terminal** (was Markets; Logan, batch 67) | Exchange tickers, charts, order entry, FX rates, commodities, bond yields, published financials and filings. Accurate for anything published (11.1.1) |
| **Intel** (Logan, batch 67) | Everything the character knows for certain: secrets, traits, real controllers, hidden holdings (11.1.1) |
| **News** (Logan, batch 67) | The feed of stories from named outlets, each with its slant; the same stories arrive as pop-ups (11.4) |
| **Search engine** (Logan, batch 67) | Search a person, company or topic to see all content created about it (11.1.1) |
| **Industries** | The 42 industries with the published demand, supply and price indices (from Pdoomberg, 11.1.1) and your business units in each |
| **Politics** | Governments, laws, ideology blocs, elections, regulators, your influence |
| **Quarterly recap** | 7 days before each earnings date: anticipated earnings, the expected market reaction, the effect on investor confidence, and the company outlook; trading in the company's shares locks for executives until the 2nd ([detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md](detailed/3-markets-and-fx.md) 3.1.0, batch 84) |
| **Event modal** | Event cards with options. Every event uses a split screen: scenario and decisions on the left; on the right, about 40% of the screen, a dense, clearly presented panel of information relevant to the event that uses all its space ([detailed/13-events.md](detailed/13-events.md) 13.2.1, batch 82) |
| **Top bar** | Date and hour, pause and 5 speed controls, skip-ahead, alert settings, cash, Clout, Fame and Favor, alerts |

## 11.3 Map rendering

- World map from simplified vector country and region shapes (public domain data such as Natural Earth), drawn as Godot 2D graphics.
- Hubs are nodes with district grids at hub zoom.
- Trade routes drawn as lines with animated flow; chokepoints highlighted.

## 11.4 Look and feel (Logan, 2026-10-17)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Art style | Flat vector UI with a **neon-on-dark cyberpunk palette** and illustrated character portraits. |
| Portraits | **Layered 2D portraits built from parts** (face, hair, clothes, accessories), which age with the character. Health and addiction show subtly (tired eyes, weight change) **(A)**. |
| Main screen | **The world map**, with panels sliding over it, like Crusader Kings. The Portfolio, Company, Character and other screens in 11.2 open as panels over the map. |
| News | **Changed (Logan, batch 67): news comes from named, biased outlets**, through pop-ups and the News screen (11.1.1). Earlier rule: **event pop-ups only**: news that matters arrives as event cards and alerts (auto-pause rules in [09-time-and-turns.md](09-time-and-turns.md) 9.1). There is no scrolling news ticker. The phone tab's feed stays only as the doomscrolling vice and its rumors ([detailed/11-addiction.md](detailed/11-addiction.md)) **(A)**. |
| Sound | **Minimal or none** for now. |

**Palette (A).** Near-black background (#0B0D12), panel grey (#161A22), text off-white (#E6E8EE), with neon accents used for meaning: cyan for money and gains, magenta for alerts and losses, amber for warnings, green for health and growth. Contrast meets WCAG AA for all text.
