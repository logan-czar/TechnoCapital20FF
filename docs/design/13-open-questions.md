# 13. Open questions and assumptions

## Decided by Logan (2026-10-16)

- Lightweight 2D UI, built in Godot as a desktop app (batch 41; Phaser dropped).
- First build focus: company ownership, corporate feudalism, RPG and CK3-style systems.
- Charters decide company heads; default head is President and CEO with majority voting control or leading the majority bloc.
- Bloc leader is the member with the most shares in the bloc.
- Vassals are held by companies. Types include subsidiary, branch office, shell company, with more to come.
- The board works like the CK3 council.
- The family trust replaces gold and carries the dynasty with set rules across the playthrough; characters can also hold assets in personal trusts and accounts with no restrictions.
- Characters are landless; assets are titles.
- No founder charter: Found Company with founding paths (VC, Ask Daddy, own cash, loan, freestyle, political connections), each with its own event chain.
- Influence is rooted mainly in assets owned in an area, modified by origin and temporarily boosted by psyops or ad campaigns.
- Turn-based, one turn per hour, with some calculations on weekly and monthly intervals.
- The full game includes the map, economy, markets and politics.
- Tone: grounded corporate drama with cyberpunk accents.
- Economy works like Victoria 3, except companies own or lease the buildings they produce in.
- Real estate: buy a plot or a series of plots, then develop it, rent it out, keep it, or use it for a company.
- The game has a private jet and travel system.
- Disclosure lines at 10%, 25% and 50% are the same everywhere; securities law only sets the first filing line (2% to 10%).
- Politics has a Victoria 2 style ideology system with nine cyberpunk ideologies: Liberals, Communists, Accelerationists, Effective Altruists, Transhumanists, Neo-Luddites, Sovereigntists, Landites and Chuds ([detailed/5-ideology.md](detailed/5-ideology.md)).
- Techno-Capital (compute, foundries, lithography machines, drones, automated cars and similar) must be allocated to tasks with Techno-Capital requirements for them to run or finish. The AI, data center and semiconductor supply chain is key, especially in the early game ([detailed/6-technocapital.md](detailed/6-technocapital.md)).
- Techno-Capital is leased, not sold (the machines themselves can be bought and sold). Control levels: Owned (you own it), Leased (pay an agreed amount for an agreed time), Spot (expensive, on demand), Compromised (secretly using someone else's TC while they use it or it is idle).
- Game type (Logan, 2026-10-17): a 4X game with **no stages**. Play is shaped by **scenarios**; there is one, starting in 2022. Stage files are retired.
- Starting world (Logan, 2026-10-16 and 17): the player picks one of **15 premade starting characters** in three groups (Americas; Europe, Africa and the Middle East; Asia and Oceania). Characters vary: some are rich with a secret or burden, some are well connected but not wealthy, some are average with really good stats. Logan's characters include Antone Provolone (New York), Ricky Feng (San Francisco), Dot Percy (Los Angeles), John Oligarch (Calgary) and Pablo Segundo (Medellín). The world holds 30 or more fully original dynasties mixing old money, new tech money, state-linked families and criminal money, each with an Origin city and a Center of Power (the hub where it has the most influence; influence is money, power and prestige: assets are the base, and power and prestige modify it). New dynasties rise from AI founders and dead ones drop out. Full design: [detailed/7-starting-world.md](detailed/7-starting-world.md).
- Opening quest (Logan, 2026-10-16): the first quest is getting the COVID vaccine or a doctor's note excusing you. It introduces the **quest system**. Getting the vaccine is easy; avoiding it is harder, by an amount that depends on the character and on rolls through the quest tree. Finishing the quest unlocks travel and other parts of the game, and delivers a tip naming which company will make the first LLM breakthrough and explaining how the AI system will work before the AI boom. **Superseded 2026-10-17**: COVID never happened in the game world, so the opening quest is now "Papers" (a flagged travel profile, cleared properly or through a fixer), keeping the travel lock and the tip.
- Timeline 2022 to 2030 (Logan, 2026-10-16): the pandemic is mostly over at the start. History follows the real AI boom, with more and more fantastical things happening as the years pass. In 2030 a crisis hits and the AI bubble pops; the game continues into a post-crash era with new rules. Real events happen on schedule by default and players can change them.
- The LLM tip from the opening quest is always correct, and the tipped lab is different each game.
- Quests are tracked goal trees with branches and rolls; events are single pop-ups that can start a quest.
- Until the opening quest is finished the player can go anywhere, but only by car or train (no flights).
- Succession (Logan, 2026-10-17): no CK3-style heirs. The player plays one character. The family trust and personal trusts can be left to other characters, and changed during life within each trust's rules (details later). On death the player picks a successor (the one named in the will is shown first); for a successor outside the family the player chooses each time whether to switch families; minors can succeed with a guardian; the game ends with no successor or at an optional end date. Eligible successors: children, spouse, business partners, mentees, or people the player owes a large favor or money. The player continues as that character with their share of the trusts and Techno-Capital.
- Strata (Logan, 2026-10-17): Victoria 3 style strata, meaning income levels, not hero classes. Every named character has a stratum set by their character resources: assets, clout, income, prestige and vril. Strata: **Permanent Underclass** (debt spiral, docile if slop needs are met), **PMC** (skilled union labor to low-level executives), **Glitterati** (top executives, influencers, athletes with field skills; rarely leverage Techno-Capital), **Nobles** (bloodline clout that fades unless descendants achieve). Detail: [detailed/2-map-influence-economy.md](detailed/2-map-influence-economy.md) 2.8.0.
- Named characters (Logan, 2026-10-17): **table characters** fill rosters; **full characters** live in the world (run companies, hold jobs, run for office). The number of full characters is set at game start, 200 by default, spread across backgrounds, strata and locations: unpicked starting characters, some rivals from the rival rotation, and random characters from high-variance templates. Detail: [detailed/1-corporate-core.md](detailed/1-corporate-core.md) 1.1.0.
- Scenario starts January 2022. Antone Provolone is a finance bro by day and a wise guy by night. Pablo Segundo is Talented. Unpicked starting characters: some are active rivals and some passive, depending on who the player picks.
- Character resources (Logan, 2026-10-17): **vril** is an esoteric X factor that does not follow regular game rules and sometimes punishes, sometimes helps the player. **Influence** is a character's PR: **Fame** (how many people know you) and **Favor** (how much they like you). **Clout** is a ticker of achievements, a permanent buff for how much you have done. Strata are set mainly by assets, capital and income, with criteria per stratum (an Oligarch sits on the board of a monopoly or holds $1B in assets, plus a certain amount of Techno-Capital). The **rival rotation** is a roster of preset-AI characters added to every game that can be directed at the player or others. Table characters can become full characters (details later).
- Batch 11 (Logan, 2026-10-17): influence keeps its map meaning; the PR resource (Fame and Favor) gets another name (Image, proposed). Strata bottom to top: Permanent Underclass, PMC, Glitterati, Nobles, Oligarchs. Rival rotation characters are aimed by the director AI and by characters who hire them, the player included. Vril works like luck: gained by vril-worthy acts and events, never clearly defined.
- Quests (Logan, 2026-10-17): each starting character has a long main quest spine to 2030 with side quests; failure branches into new quests; only the next step is visible; AI-run starting characters sometimes play out their quest lines; quests also come from events, requests and favors, rival rotation schemes and the timeline. Detail: [10-events.md](10-events.md) 10.4.
- Quest lines (Logan, 2026-10-17): quest lines are made of **arcs** (not chapters); the number, length and structure vary; arcs can run concurrently; outcomes give better or worse stat buffs. Every character gets a placeholder default line (tutorial opening, then milestones) except Pieter Von Floop, whose line teaches running a massive public company and managing wealth in 4 to 6 arcs. The crash hits in 2030 on average, varying by a few years with RNG and events. Ricky Feng's wallet quest is deferred; he still starts with compute in Asia and a bot farm SaaS. Detail: [detailed/8-quest-lines.md](detailed/8-quest-lines.md).
- Design order (Logan, 2026-10-17): flesh out the economy, corporations and investing first, then character stats and personalities; scenarios and quest lines come last.
- Batch 15 (Logan, 2026-10-17): the player can run investment funds with outside money (hedge, VC, private equity; already in markets 3.17 and 3.18). Private stakes trade through secondaries, funding rounds and SPACs. New company types: holding companies and cooperatives (joint ventures and franchises already exist). Crypto is shallow: coins move randomly in boom and bust cycles, inside a deep gambling system (phone tab: prediction markets on game-world events, crypto casinos) that is part of the addiction system ([detailed/9-gambling.md](detailed/9-gambling.md)). The ~60 goods stay, but basic necessities like food and clothing are oversaturated with no market; population needs are consumer goods. The Permanent Underclass needs only **slop**: a byproduct of all company production, not sold, consumed locally, never imported or exported; too little makes an area unstable, too much hurts education, health and birth rate. Influence is not a skill (it is influence in an area); the skill is renamed Persuasion.
- Batch 16 (Logan, 2026-10-17): the PMC still needs housing, energy and healthcare. Only tech and industry are simulated at the start; starter goods look like the real economy (phones, PCs, cars, electric cars, GPUs, steel, oil and similar). The GPU supply chain is the largest supply chain. The addiction system covers gambling, drugs, cyberware, doomscrolling and AI psychosis. No risk for betting on events you control. Companies do not choose slop: each good's recipe has a slop ratio (slop per good made).
- GPU chain (Logan, 2026-10-17): at the start only **energy and GPUs** are modeled; everything else is abstracted. GPUs are components that go into PCs and AI server nodes; fabless designers own designs; a developer ecosystem score steers buyers; consumers and AI compete for the same cards, with AI demand starting very low and growing much faster than steady consumer demand. Simulated as four layers ending in the GPU: lithography machines (extremely expensive: $200M per EUV machine), wafers, chips, GPUs. Detail: [detailed/6-technocapital.md](detailed/6-technocapital.md) 6.7.0.
- Batch 18 (Logan, 2026-10-17): energy means electricity plus crude oil and natural gas as modeled fuels. The order for switching abstracted goods to full simulation is still open. Four or more GPU designers at the start. The player can found a GPU designer, but it is prohibitively expensive in most market conditions. Boom speed varies by save, and the boom has a triggering event.
- Batch 19 (Logan, 2026-10-17): the four GPU layers end with the GPU itself and keep the chain simple; the lithography machine is in the chain and extremely expensive. The boom trigger is always the tipped lab's breakthrough, which players can make come earlier or later. Oil and gas get their own chokepoints and shocks (Hormuz, cartel cuts, sanctions on Russia, the Red Sea). The GPU chain gets its own UI screen. Next topic: corporations and investing.
- Economy model (Logan, 2026-10-17): the Victoria 3 goods model is scrapped for Wall Street Raider style economic modeling: industries per country, business units that win market share up to capacity, and company-level levers (expand, shrink, stance, buy, sell, merge, spin off). Detail: [detailed/10-industry-economy.md](detailed/10-industry-economy.md). The switch-on order question is moot.
- Batch 21 (Logan, 2026-10-17): a business unit is one building or site; energy and GPUs are measured in dollars like every other industry (price indices, no unit counts); about 36 industries; running sites is mostly automatic, with the player making deals (buy, sell, merge, build, close, spin off); a company can IPO once it meets its exchange's listing rules and hires an underwriter; stock trading is quote-based with slippage plus limit and stop orders.
- Batch 22 (Logan, 2026-10-17): no random, cyclical management rating like Wall Street Raider; a company's management score comes from the stats of the characters in its board positions and sets investor confidence and productivity. A site is like an office that holds a percentage of the company; companies grow by buying or leasing real estate to expand a site (on the balance sheet), and target sites to grow or areas for new sites; site size in an area gives buffs such as lower interest rates and higher productivity. AI companies make deals by archetype; private companies are valued by last round or an earnings multiple; companies that cannot pay go through a restructuring event chain.
- Batch 23 Q1 (Logan, 2026-10-17): a company works in one industry. Real estate: each location has a set pool of commercial and residential real estate; investing buys part of the pool, which appreciates; owners choose how much to rent out for passive income; real estate has its own tax rates; renting a site rents from the location's commercial pool at a rate set by available stock and rental demand. ## Assumptions to confirm

1. **Geography**: the real modern world (real countries and cities) with fictional companies, characters and brands.
2. **Charter types**: Standard, Dual Leadership, Board-Governed and State-Linked are enough for now.
3. **Influence thresholds**: 10%, 25% and 50% share of a place's weighted assets.
4. **Player scope**: the player directly controls the dynasty head's actions, personal accounts and the family trust; other family members act on their own.
5. **Starting scenario**: decided (see above): one 2022 scenario with 15 starting characters.
6. **Wars**: countries go to war as background events; players never command armies (PMCs dropped, batch 51).
