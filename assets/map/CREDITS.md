## Natural Earth

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com. Version v5.1.2 (github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, commit f1890d9): admin 0 countries, admin 1 states and provinces, populated places and lakes at 1:10m. Public domain.
